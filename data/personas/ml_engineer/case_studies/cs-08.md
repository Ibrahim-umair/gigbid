A host managing 46 short-term rental properties answered the same guest questions at all hours: Wi-Fi password, parking, bin day, late check-in, where the nearest pharmacy is. The host's house manual existed as one long PDF per property. I created a guest assistant that works over ordinary SMS and over WhatsApp, both via Twilio, so guests do not install anything. Each property's manual is parsed into question-and-answer snippets, and the assistant answers only from the snippets for the property linked to the guest's phone number and booking dates.

If a guest reports something urgent (no hot water, lockout) or the assistant is unsure, it replies that the host has been notified and sends a summary to the host's phone, with a two-minute escalation timer. After checkout, the guest gets one polite message asking for a review.

Over the first season, 71% of guest messages were resolved without the host, the median reply took 8 seconds, and the host reported saving about 11 hours per week. The average review score for the properties rose from 4.5 to 4.7. Guest numbers are deleted 30 days after checkout.
