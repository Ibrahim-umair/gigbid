A mid-sized estate agency in a coastal city was losing buyers because enquiries arriving after 6 pm waited until the next morning, a median of four hours. I built a conversational assistant on WhatsApp using the Twilio WhatsApp Business API and an LLM prompted with the agency's listing data. The bot greets the lead, asks about budget, bedrooms, move-in date and financing status, and answers questions about active listings from a synced inventory table. Session state lives in Redis with a 24-hour window so conversations survive the WhatsApp reply limits.

When a lead scores as qualified (budget fits and a viewing date is named) the bot books a viewing slot in the agents' shared calendar and pings the right agent. Anything about pricing negotiation or legal terms is handed to a human with the transcript attached.

Over the first quarter the bot handled about 1,100 conversations a week. Median first response dropped from 4 hours to 20 seconds, 38% of inbound leads were qualified without agent time, and booked viewings rose 27%. Opt-out requests were honoured automatically and stored for compliance.
