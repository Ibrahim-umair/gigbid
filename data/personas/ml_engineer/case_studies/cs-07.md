An online academy with 120 recorded lessons saw learners drop off at the first difficult module. I transcribed all videos with Whisper, cleaned and chunked the transcripts by topic, and built two features on top. The first is a companion chat that answers questions using only the lesson material and links to the exact timestamp of the explanation. The second generates five-question quizzes per lesson with explanations for every wrong answer.

Retrieval was a plain embedding search with a small reranker; I deliberately avoided orchestration frameworks, because direct API calls kept the prompt flow easy for the academy's in-house developer to maintain. Quiz questions were generated in two passes, draft then self-critique, and a teacher reviewed 300 sampled items: 92% were accepted unchanged and 6% needed light edits.

In the cohort that had access to the companion, module-two completion rose from 54% to 64% (an 18% relative increase) over eight weeks, and the median learner asked 3.2 questions per lesson. The total running cost was about $85 a month for roughly 1,900 active learners.
