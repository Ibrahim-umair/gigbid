A hotel group with 19 properties had 48,000 guest reviews across booking sites and wanted to know what drove low scores, without reading them all. I fine-tuned a multilingual transformer from the Hugging Face hub for aspect-level sentiment on 3,000 reviews labelled by the group's own guest-relations team, covering eight aspects such as cleanliness, noise, breakfast, staff and check-in.

The macro F1 on the held-out reviews was 0.82 for aspect detection and 0.89 for polarity. Reviews in Dutch, German and French were handled by the same model, which avoided translating anything.

I then aggregated the output with pandas into a per-property monthly view and delivered a short analysis. Noise complaints at two city-centre properties accounted for 37% of their negative reviews, and breakfast queue times explained a dip at a third hotel every weekend. The group moved the breakfast start by 30 minutes and added a second coffee station; that property's breakfast sentiment improved by 19 points over the next quarter. The pipeline runs weekly as a scheduled job, and new reviews appear in the shared workbook the guest-relations team already uses.
