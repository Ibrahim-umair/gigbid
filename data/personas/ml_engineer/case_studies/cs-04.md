A boutique law firm reviewed about 3,200 supplier contracts a year and wanted the paralegals to see risky clauses (termination, indemnity, auto-renewal, liability caps and 20 other types) highlighted before reading. I fine-tuned a legal-domain BERT checkpoint from the Hugging Face hub on 5,400 paragraphs the firm had already annotated, and added a lightweight rules layer for dates and monetary caps.

On a held-out set of 600 paragraphs the model reached a macro F1 of 0.87 across 24 clause types, against 0.71 for the keyword baseline the firm had been using. The weakest class was "limitation of liability" at 0.78, where I added 300 more examples through active learning and gained five points.

The model was deployed behind an async HTTP endpoint with request batching and a warm worker, so a 40-page contract is processed in about 9 seconds on a single T4 GPU. A small review UI, built by the firm's own developer, calls this endpoint and shows highlights next to the original text.

Average first-pass review time per contract dropped from 45 minutes to 12, and no high-risk clause flagged by senior counsel in the pilot month was missed.
